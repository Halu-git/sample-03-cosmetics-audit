# Glossary — Japanese statistics & retail terms used in this report

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning in this report |
|---|---|---|
| 生産動態統計調査 | Seisan Dōtai Tōkei | METI's Current Survey of Production — monthly production/shipment/inventory statistics by product, incl. cosmetics |
| 工場出荷金額 | kōjō shukka kingaku | Factory shipment value — value of goods shipped from domestic factories (incl. for export). **Not** retail market size |
| 商業動態統計調査 | Shōgyō Dōtai Tōkei | METI's Current Survey of Commerce — monthly retail/wholesale sales by format, incl. drugstores |
| ビューティケア（化粧品・小物） | byūtikea | "Beauty care (cosmetics & sundries)" — the cosmetics category in drugstore sales statistics |
| 貿易統計 | Bōeki Tōkei | Trade Statistics of Japan (Ministry of Finance / Customs) |
| 家計調査 | Kakei Chōsa | Family Income and Expenditure Survey (FIES), MIC — benchmark household survey |
| 訪日外国人消費動向調査／インバウンド消費動向調査 | — | Inbound Consumption Trend Survey, Japan Tourism Agency (renamed in 2024) |
| 医薬部外品 | iyaku-bugaihin | "Quasi-drugs" — a PMD Act category between cosmetics and pharmaceuticals (e.g., medicated whitening products); requires approval and permits specified efficacy claims |
| 薬機法 | Yakki-hō | PMD Act — the act governing pharmaceuticals, medical devices, cosmetics and quasi-drugs in Japan |
| ドラッグストア | doraggu-sutoa | Drugstore — Japan's dominant mass channel for cosmetics (large chains also sell food and daily goods) |
| バラエティストア | — | Variety/lifestyle stores (e.g., Loft, Plaza) — discovery-oriented beauty retail |
| クチコミ | kuchikomi | Word-of-mouth / user reviews (the basis of @cosme's rankings) |
